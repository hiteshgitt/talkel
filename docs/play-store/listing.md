## Full description (max 4000)

Nervous about a job interview in English? Asking for a raise? Or just want to talk confidently with anyone?

Talkel lets you practise the real conversation first — on a voice call with an AI partner who listens, replies and reacts just like a person. Then you get clear feedback on exactly what to fix.

TALK, DON'T TAP
• A real voice call, not flashcards or multiple choice
• Natural back-and-forth: interrupt, pause, think — your partner keeps up
• Four AI partners — Maya, Rohan, Priya and Arjun — with Indian, American, British or Australian accents
• Choose your level, from beginner to advanced

PRACTISE THE MOMENTS THAT MATTER
• Job interviews — including the job you are applying for or doing right now
• Missions with rising pressure: get the job, negotiate your raise, calm an angry customer, face an angry manager, pitch your idea, pass a visa interview, rebook a cancelled flight
• Everyday English: friendly chats, making a new friend, bargaining like a local, fixing a hotel problem, client meetings and debates

YOUR OWN TOPICS
• Describe the exact conversation you have coming up — in English, Hindi or Hinglish
• Talkel turns it into a practice call: your manager, landlord, a client or a new friend
• Choose how they behave: friendly, realistic or tough
• Practise it again and again until you feel ready

FEEDBACK YOU CAN USE — IN ENGLISH OR HINDI
• After every call, see what went well and what to improve
• Grammar mistakes explained, with the better way to say it
• New words and phrases to use next time
• Fluency: speaking pace, pauses and how quickly you answer
• Read the full transcript of your call
• "Try that answer again" — replay one moment and see how much better you did

WATCH YOURSELF IMPROVE
• Scores and progress charts over time
• Practice suggestions based on your weak spots
• Talkel remembers details you share, so conversations feel personal (you can view and delete them any time)

FREE TO START
Practise for 10 minutes every day, and create your first custom topic, free.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your conversations are yours. Delete any conversation, recording or memory — or your whole account — at any time, from the app.

Built in India for Indian learners: students, freshers, job seekers and working professionals who know English but want to speak it with confidence.

Talkel is for users aged 18 and over.
